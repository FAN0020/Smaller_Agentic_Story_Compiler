The Lion and the Mouse

A lion slept in the shade after a long hunt. A small mouse ran across
his paw and woke him.

The lion caught the mouse beneath one great paw.

“Please let me go,” cried the mouse. “Perhaps one day I may help you.”

The lion laughed at the idea that such a tiny creature could help him,
but he released it.

Days later, hunters caught the lion in a rope net. He struggled, but
every movement tightened the knots. His roar carried through the forest.

The mouse heard him and hurried to the trap. It gnawed one rope, then
another, until the lion could tear himself free.

The lion looked at the mouse with new respect.

“No friend is too small to matter,” he said.

Moral: Kindness is never wasted.
