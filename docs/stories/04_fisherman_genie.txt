The Fisherman and the Genie

An old fisherman lived with his family beside the sea. Each morning he
cast his net only four times.

One morning his first cast brought rubbish. His second brought broken
pottery. His third brought stones and mud.

Before the fourth cast he asked fortune for something useful.

The net returned with a heavy brass jar sealed with metal. Hoping it
contained treasure, the fisherman opened it.

Smoke poured out, climbed into the sky, and formed an enormous genie.

“Prepare to die,” said the genie.

The fisherman stared at him. “I freed you.”

The genie explained that he had been imprisoned for centuries. At first
he had promised riches to whoever released him. Later he promised
kingdoms and magical power. But no rescuer came. Hope became anger, and
eventually he swore that whoever freed him would die.

The fisherman was frightened, but he began to think.

“You expect me to believe you fitted inside that jar?”

The genie was offended.

“You are larger than a house,” the fisherman continued. “Your hand would
not fit through the opening.”

“Watch.”

The genie dissolved into smoke. The cloud spiraled downward until all of
it had entered the jar.

“Do you believe me now?” called a voice from inside.

The fisherman immediately replaced the seal.

The jar shook.

“Open it!”

“No. A moment ago you planned my death. Perhaps I should return you to
the sea.”

The genie threatened him, then pleaded, then promised rewards.

The fisherman did not hurry. Only after the genie swore a solemn oath
not to harm him did he open the jar again.

The genie emerged and kept his promise.

He led the fisherman inland to a hidden lake where fish of remarkable
colors moved beneath the water.

“Cast your net here,” he said. “Take your catch to your ruler, but fish
here only once each day.”

Then he vanished.

The fisherman caught four extraordinary fish and carried them to the
palace. Their appearance astonished the ruler, who rewarded him and
demanded to know where they came from.

The hidden lake proved to be connected to an enchanted kingdom and a
prince trapped by magic.

The fisherman had gone to sea hoping only to feed his family. By
surviving the genie through patience and wit, he had opened the door to
a much larger adventure.
